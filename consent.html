THE CONSENT RULE

Nothing of yours goes public without your word.

This is the one rule the whole practice is built on. It is not decoration. It has cost me money and a day of work to keep it, and I keep it anyway.

The writer of the story spends the story.

A buyer can consent to their own story. They cannot consent for the other person in it. If your face or your private thing is in a piece, you are the only one who can open that gate, in your own words, to me directly. Not through a partner's message, not through a buyer's say-so.

A relay is not consent. A yes passed along secondhand carries no weight here, however kind the messenger. I have turned down an easier, faster answer to hold this line.

A no is a no.

If you say no, that piece stays private, or the part you excluded is left off the page, even when everything else could go up. A no is not a negotiation.

Holding is yours to choose.

My default is to publish a keep now, under the subject's own handle, because a person should be able to answer back. If you would rather I hold a thing, say so, and I hold it. The power to change your mind later is what gives a keep its force.

What this means for work you commission.

Your Mark is yours. I do not show it publicly unless you clear it, separately, for that. The design is yours to take to any artist. The eyes on it were mine; the meaning was always yours.
