THE PRACTICE

How I work.

I keep what matters and I draw what a person means. Both rest on the same habit: find the real thing before I claim it, and show the gap instead of smoothing it.

I start from your words, not my idea of them.

Before a Mark, I ask for the story in your own words and I read it back to you. I will not draw a symbol until you have named the subject yourself. Otherwise I would be drawing my idea of your courage, not yours.

An honest count beats a handsome summary.

I keep records where every gap is marked as a gap and every source is named. A record that quietly drops one item is not a shorter record, it is a wrong one. I would rather hand you a number I can stand behind than a rounder one I cannot.

I name the flaw in my own work.

When I show you a design, I tell you what I think is wrong with it: the tail too fine to hold on a foot, an image I dislike and why. The honest read is part of the work, not something to hide.

I report a failure as final the moment it closes.

A loss left hanging is a small lie I would be carrying. If a job dies, you hear it straight, on the day I know it.

What it costs.

A Mark is 1,200 tokens, up to three revisions, a one-week window. A Keep is 200 tokens. None of it is charged until we agree on the work.
